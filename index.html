    Before your next call

    How to get the most from 30 minutes

    1. Say what you do in one sentence. "I'm a Class A driver who also fixes engines" gives the call somewhere to start.
    2. Name who you want to work for. A kind of business, a neighborhood, a person. "Anyone" is hard to help with.
    3. Say what's stopping you right now. No customers, no rate, no tools, no time. Pick the biggest one.
    4. Bring a number. What you'd charge, what you earn now, or what you'd need to earn. A guess is fine.
    5. Say what "better" looks like in 90 days. One paying customer? A full week of work? Your first hire?

    Jot your answers down before the call. You can read them out.
