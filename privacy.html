One Percent · Privacy

What is kept, and who handles it.

Version 2026-10-12. Paying for a session means agreeing to this page.

Paying

Paying needs no account, name or email. Kept: how you paid, the amount, your 10-character reference, and a scrambled form of your order link. The link itself is not kept, so only you hold it.

A gift card code is kept encrypted until the payment is checked, then deleted. A scrambled form of it stays, so the same card can't be used twice and so you can find your order with it.

A Wise payment is made in Wise. Wise shows the amount, the reference and the name on the sending account, as it does for any transfer. That name is not copied into One Percent.

Signing in

Signing in is done through Skills Economy. One Percent receives your account number and the name on the account, and keeps the name encrypted.

Cookies are for signing in only and last 12 hours. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Your call and your work

Calls run through Retell, a voice service. Retell carries the audio, writes the transcript and sends it here. Kept, encrypted: the transcript, a summary and the call's record.

Also kept, encrypted: messages between you and Farah, your plan and steps, the people you name and what passed between you, and Farah's notes on working with you.

If you have a Skills Economy Directory profile, Farah can read it to make introductions. Nothing from here goes back to Skills Economy.

AI drafts

Drafts are written by open models run on rented servers (RunPod), not by an AI company's service. Your material is sent there to draft from, and the draft comes back. It is not used to train any model.

What is learned about your trade

What the work teaches about doing a trade well is kept and used to help other people in that trade. It is kept as advice about the trade, never as anything about you: no name, place, words of yours, figures or detail that points back to you.

It is One Percent's own work, like the method. Because it holds nothing of yours, it stays if you leave.

Who else handles it

  • Neon holds the database.
  • Render runs the app, in the United States.
  • Retell carries calls.
  • RunPod runs the AI drafts.

No analytics, no advertising, no email or text messages. Nothing is sold or passed to anyone not listed here.

How long

Gift card codes are deleted once checked, and drafting work files are cleared within 7 days. Everything else is kept while One Percent runs.

When this page changes, its version changes, and the next payment asks again.
